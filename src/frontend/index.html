    10 simple steps to protect your business

    1. Check before you send money. If a message asks you to pay or to change bank details, call the person first. Use a phone number you already have. Never use the number in the message.
    2. Do not let fear rush you. Scammers say "pay now or else." Real banks, courts and the tax office do not rush you. Hang up. Call the number on your bill.
    3. Make a family safe word. Pick a secret word with your family and staff. If a caller asks for money and cannot say the word, it is not them. Voices and video can be faked now.
    4. Do not click links in texts. Open the real app or type the real website yourself. Never read a code from your phone to anyone.
    5. Use a different password for every account. Keep them in a password manager app. Turn on two-step login, starting with your email.
    6. Lock your phone and computer. Use a screen lock. Turn on "find my phone." Do not do banking on free public Wi-Fi.
    7. Keep cameras and smart devices apart. Put them on your router's guest Wi-Fi. Change the password on the first day.
    8. Ask every visitor who they are. If a repair person comes and you did not call them, call the company first. Use a number you already have.
    9. Keep 3 copies of important files. Keep one copy away from the shop and not connected. Let updates install by themselves at night.
    10. Make a plan for a bad day. Tell your staff: "Tell me fast if something goes wrong. You will not be in trouble."

    Got hacked or scammed? Do this now.

    1. If money was sent, call your bank right away.
    2. Tell your staff and any customers who may be at risk.
    3. Change your passwords, starting with your email.
    4. Write down what happened, with dates and times.
    5. Report it to the FBI at ic3.gov.
